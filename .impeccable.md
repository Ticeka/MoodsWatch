## Design Context

### Users
MoodToon is for anime, manga, and webtoon fans who want to discover what to watch or read next, track progress, and shape recommendations around their taste. The core experience should feel lightweight and welcoming for casual browsing, but still support deeper collection management for returning users.

### Brand Personality
Cute, friendly, playful. The interface should feel optimistic, soft, and approachable rather than intense, futuristic, or overly serious. It should invite exploration, reward curiosity, and make list management feel charming instead of administrative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a bright, editorial-playful direction inspired by the warmth and friendliness of EasyTomorrow's site rather than generic anime dashboards. Favor soft paper-like backgrounds, rounded and tactile controls, cheerful accents, charming motion, and layouts with personality. Avoid neon cyber UI, dark gamer aesthetics, sterile SaaS cards, and flat monochrome dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with warmth: every major surface should feel welcoming, soft, and human.
2. Make browsing playful: discovery flows should feel like exploration, not filtering through a database.
3. Keep hierarchy obvious: playful does not mean noisy; key actions and content should stay easy to scan.
4. Use characterful details sparingly: rounded shapes, hand-touched accents, and gentle motion should add charm without hurting readability.
5. Preserve utility: watchlist, progress, and recommendation controls must remain fast and dependable.
